Afking

Use the AFK feature when you step away so other players know you are not responding.

How it works

  1. Type /afk before you leave.
  2. Type /afk again or just move to return.

Commands

CommandDescription
/afkToggle your AFK status

Tips

  • Players who are longer than one hour afk, will be teleported to spawn.
  • Other players can see if you're AFK if they see a red dot after your name when you press TAB.
