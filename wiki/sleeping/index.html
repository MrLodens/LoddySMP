Sleeping

You do not need everyone in bed to skip the night. When enough players are sleeping, the night is skipped and the weather clears.

How it works

  1. Find a bed and right-click it at night.
  2. Wait for other players to join you.
  3. When enough players sleep, it becomes day.

Tips

  • Set your spawn by sleeping in a bed, so you respawn at home.
  • Ask in chat or on Discord if you want someone to sleep with you.
