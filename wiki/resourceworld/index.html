Resource World

The Resource World is a world meant for mining and gathering. It is reset from time to time, so you can find fresh resources while the main world stays untouched.

How it works

  1. Travel to the Resource World using /rtp and select the right world.
  2. Explore and gather the resources you need.

Tips

  • Do not build your base in the Resource World.
  • The Resource World will reset every beginning of the month. This will be announced in our Discord
  • Every month there's a new dragon egg to obtain.
