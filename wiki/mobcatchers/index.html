Mobcatchers

Mobcatchers let you catch a mob and carry it in an item. Use it to move animals to your base or farm.

How it works

  1. Hold the mobcatcher and use it on a mob to catch it.
  2. Move to where you want it.
  3. Use the item on the ground to release the mob again.

Tips

  • Keep your mobcatcher safe; the mob inside is gone if you lose the item.
  • Not every mob can be caught.
