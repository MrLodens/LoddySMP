Lands

Lands lets you claim chunks to protect your builds from griefing and theft. Several players can join the same land, so you can build together.

How it works

  1. Stand in the area you want to protect.
  2. Create your land with /lands create <name>.
  3. Claim the chunk you are standing in with /lands claim.
  4. Invite friends with /lands invite <player>.

Commands

CommandDescription
/lands create <name>Create a new land
/lands claimClaim the chunk you are standing in
/lands unclaimRelease the chunk you are standing in
/lands invite <player>Invite a player to your land
/lands trust <player>Give a player access to your land without joining it
/lands untrust <player>Remove a player from your land
/lands infoShow information about the land you are in
/lands menuOpen the land menu
/lands helpList all available commands
/lands claim autoAutomatically claim chunks while you walk (use /lands unclaim auto to unclaim)
/lands mapShow a map of the claims around you (/lands map chat or /lands map link)
/lands viewShow the borders of nearby claims (/lands view here, /lands view stay, /lands view disable)
/lands deleteDelete the active land
/lands editChange between lands

Tips

  • Use /lands area or the menu to manage roles and settings of your land.
  • Use /lands map to see what is already claimed before you claim.
  • Use /lands edit to claim chunks in another land.
  • Only give trusted players access; they can build in your land.
