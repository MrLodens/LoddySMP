PvP

Want to fight it out with your rivals? Fight eachother in the PvP Arena

How it works

  1. Travel to the PvP Arena using /warp PvP.
  2. Fight for your motherland.

Tips

  • Keep inventory is enabled in our arena.
  • Your items won't lose any durability.
  • Building is disabled inside the arena.
