Chest Shops

You can sell items to other players using a chest. Players buy by right-clicking your shop and the money goes straight to you.

How it works

  1. Place down a chest and hold the item you want to sell.
  2. Look at the chest and punch it.
  3. Enter the price in the chat.
  4. Place the specific items inside the chest.

Commands

Command Description
/qs about Show Chest Shop information
/qs amount To set the amount of items
/qs browse Browse shops that exist in this server
/qs fetchmessage Show unread shop messages
/qs item Change shop item of a shop
/qs setowner Changes the ownership of a shop
/qs toggledisplay Toggle the shop's display-item status
/qs create <price> Create a shop of the item in your hand (look at the chest)
/qs price <price> Change the price of a shop
/qs buy Change your shop to buy items from players
/qs sell Change your shop to sell items to players
/qs find <item> Find shops that sell an item
/qs remove Remove the shop you are looking at
/qs empty Empty the shop you are looking at

Tips

  • Protect the area with Lands so nobody else can open your shop chest.
  • Use /qs find to compare prices before setting yours.
