Welcome to LoddySMP

LoddySMP is a semi-vanilla Minecraft server built around community, progression, and a friendly survival experience.

Whether you're looking for a place to build, start your own shop, claim your land, play with friends, or simply enjoy a long-term survival experience, there's something for everyone on LoddySMP.

How to join

  1. Open MinecraftGo to Multiplayer and choose Add Server.
  2. Enter the IPFill in loddysmp.net as the server address. Need help? See How to connect.
  3. Read the rulesTake a minute to read the rules so everyone has fun.
  4. Say hiJoin our Discord and meet the community.
